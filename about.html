ABOUT

One simple idea: organize the waiting.

QueueLess PK is a frontend project concept where citizens book a scheduled token for a participating government office instead of spending the whole day in a physical queue.

Government partners would publish available capacity and services. Citizens would select a time, receive a token and visit according to the assigned slot.
