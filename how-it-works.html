How QueueLess PK Works

Four simple steps from booking to your office visit.

1

Choose Office

Select the government office and service.

2

Pick Time

Choose an available time slot.

3

Book Token

Pay the example Rs.10 token fee.

4

Visit

Reach the office around your assigned time.

Before QueueLess

Go to office → join physical line → wait for uncertain time → complete service.

With QueueLess

Book online → receive token → arrive near time slot → complete service.
