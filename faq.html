FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is QueueLess PK?

An academic frontend concept for booking time-based tokens for participating government offices.

Why Rs.10?

Rs.10 is only an example fee used to demonstrate the business idea. A real service would require an official pricing policy.

Does the token guarantee the service?

No. It would schedule a visit; the actual government service would still follow the department's rules.

Can a token be changed?

The demo does not implement real rescheduling. A production system could add it.

Will every government office be available?

Only offices that officially participate and integrate with the platform would appear.

Is this an official government website?

No. QueueLess PK is an academic frontend project concept.
